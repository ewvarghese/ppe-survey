PPE site survey checklist

Open form

Short answers are enough. “Not sure” is fine. Use the web form for auto-save, optional technical notes and photo attachments.

Before the visit: carry a charged phone and the required PPE. Get permission before taking photos. Do not record passwords or other access credentials.
Be clear about accuracy: the current reported figure is about 85%. Site results may differ. Agree on a pilot test; do not promise an improvement or replace normal safety checks.
Before leaving: review the answers and agree on a next action, owner and date. Submit, then use Download JSON copy on the review step to keep a backup with photos or share it with the survey owner. GitHub Pages keeps submissions in this browser, not in a central database. Do not clear browser data before backing up.
